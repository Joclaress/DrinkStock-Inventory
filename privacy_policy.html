Privacy Policy for DrinkStock

Last updated: October 25, 2026

DrinkStock ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application, DrinkStock - Inventory Manager Pro, collects, uses, and safeguards user information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your email address for authentication via Google Firebase.
  • Inventory & Sales Data: Product names, stock counts, cost prices, selling prices, and sales transaction logs are stored securely in Firebase Firestore.
  • Camera & Photo Access: Camera permission is requested solely to allow users to capture optional receipt proof photos for sales transactions. Photos remain private to your account.

2. Advertising & Analytics

We use Google AdMob to display banner ads and rewarded video ads. Google AdMob may collect and use anonymous device identifiers and usage data to serve ads. For more details, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Security

Your data is protected with industry-standard encryption provided by Firebase. Restock and management actions inside the app are protected by your custom Admin PIN.

4. Children's Privacy

DrinkStock is an inventory management tool for business owners and managers. It is not directed to children under the age of 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via email support.
